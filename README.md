# Gale Tower

Solo pixel-art builder game. Lay bricks under your feet, climb, and lean the tower into the wind.

- 5 bricks = 1 m
- Every 10 m a steel band locks the tower below and the wind shifts (stronger as you climb)
- Controls: ←/→ or A/D walk · Space/↑/W build · Esc pause · M sound · F full screen

Play: open `index.html` in any browser.
