Gale Tower

1 player · keyboard or touch

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen

Controls

← → or A DWalk along the top brick Space ↑ WHop and lay a brick under your feet Esc M FPause · sound · full screen

Every brick goes down where you stand, so where you stand is how the tower leans. 5 bricks make 1 m. Every 10 m a steel band locks everything below it and the wind shifts, getting stronger as you climb. Wind pushes the unbanded section over: lean it into the wind to balance. Watch the sway meter and the ghost brick colour. The forecast in the corner tells you the next wind before it arrives. Wind also drifts you mid-hop, so land on the brick.
